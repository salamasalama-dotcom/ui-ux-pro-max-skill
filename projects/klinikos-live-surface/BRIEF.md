# BRIEF.md · Klinik.OS

Interview: partially answered by the user (grammar, aesthetic, output format,
product definition, and five brand visuals supplied). The remaining answers
below are **self-authored** from the supplied product document and visuals,
not interviewed. Marked per question.

## The eight questions

1. **Vibe, three to five words.** *Self-authored:* precise, calm, operational,
   accountable. References from other media: an air traffic control strip
   board, a hospital handover sheet, a Braun instrument panel. Not a website.

2. **The scroll journey.** *User-directed (grammar choice), sequence
   self-authored:* the visitor lands inside a clinic's console at 08:05 on an
   ordinary Thursday and scrolls the working day forward. A patient question
   arrives and is answered. The permission layer that made that safe is opened
   and inspected. The appointment book fills. The automations run. The day
   resolves into the management panel. The security posture is shown as a
   settings surface. The last screen is the first-run form.

3. **The energy curve.** *Self-authored:* quiet at the inbox, technical and
   still at the permission layer, steadily building through the book, calm at
   the automations, highest at the panel, low and factual at security,
   resolved at setup.

4. **How should someone feel, stage by stage, and the ONE moment.** See the
   feeling curve below. The one moment: the panel, where the whole day the
   visitor just scrolled through resolves into numbers they never had to
   collect.

5. **One thing no site they have seen does.** *Self-authored:* the page's
   scrollbar is a clinic day. See the signature move.

6. **How far from premium-minimal.** *User-answered:* deep navy and electric
   blue, precise. Premium-minimal ground, but real operational density in the
   surfaces.

7. **One unbroken world, or distinct scenes.** *User-answered:* live surface
   grammar. Neither: the page is one continuous console with the visitor
   moving through its modules.

8. **Assets already held.** *User-supplied:* the product definition document
   and five social visuals establishing the navy and electric blue palette,
   the plus-mark motif, the Turkish voice, and the chat and appointment
   surfaces. No photography, no footage, and no image generation tool in this
   environment, so the page carries no photography. The live surface grammar
   forbids full-bleed photography anyway; the operable console is the imagery.

```
2  Yetki katmanı  Scepticism turning to trust   the refusal trace, run in front of them
3  Randevu defteri Relief       the 14:00 slot they watched get booked, filling itself
4  Otomasyon      Confidence    the reminders that went out with nobody sending them
5  Panel          Command       THE PEAK. the whole day resolves at once
6  Güvenlik       Assurance     plain facts, no persuasion
7  Kurulum        Resolve       an actual input, not a pitch
```

No two adjacent acts share a feeling. Act 4 ends on a single quiet line with
no panel activity: **that is the authored silence in front of the peak**, not
dead scroll.

## The peak

Act 5, the management panel, at the largest span on the page (3.4vh against a
2.4vh next-largest).

> "You scroll one clinic day, and at the end the whole day is just sitting
> there counted, and you realise nobody in the clinic did any of it."

## The tell-someone sentence

**It's the site where the scrollbar is a clinic's working day, and by the time
you reach the bottom the day has run itself.**

## Authored silence

The tail of act 4 (Otomasyon) holds one line on an otherwise empty field for
roughly a third of a viewport. Intentional. It is the silence before the panel.

## Honesty

Every number on the page is computed at runtime from the demo arrays in
`app.js`. Nothing is hardcoded as a business claim, and no outcome statistic
is asserted. The clinic, the patients, and the day are demonstration data and
the page says so in its status bar and in the panel footer.
